How to study each lesson

  1. Read the idea, then redo the worked example by hand on paper before looking at the code.
  2. Run the examples. Change one number and predict what the output will do before pressing Run.
  3. Answer the Check yourself questions without running anything.
  4. Do the exercise. Early exercises use plain Python on purpose: writing the loops yourself is how the rules stick. Later ones use NumPy, like CSE 6040.
  5. Tick I understand this when you could explain the lesson to a classmate in two sentences.

Each lesson has a priority tag. Essential concepts appear directly in CSE 6040 notebooks, exams, or ISYE models. Important ones power specific topics. Intuition lessons make the rest make sense but are tested less.

What analytics actually uses

Lessons build on each other, so go in order. If time runs short, finish every Essential lesson first and skim the rest. The CSE 6040 syllabus names linear algebra as one of the two gaps students struggle with most.

What you can safely skip for now

  • Proofs. Analytics courses test whether you can compute and interpret, not prove theorems.
  • Computing determinants and inverses of big matrices by hand. Know what they mean; let NumPy compute them.
  • Cross products, abstract vector spaces, complex eigenvalues, Jordan form. These rarely appear in data analysis.
  • Row-reduction drills beyond 3×3. Know the idea of solving Ax = b; np.linalg.solve does the work.

Watch

  • 3Blue1Brown: Essence of Linear Algebra. Short animated videos and the best place to build intuition. Each lesson here names the chapter that matches.
  • StatQuest. Search "PCA step by step" and "linear regression" for the analytics side.
  • MIT 18.06SC with Gilbert Strang. Full lectures if you want to go deeper later.

Read and practice

  • Introduction to Applied Linear Algebra: Vectors, Matrices, and Least Squares (Boyd and Vandenberghe). Free PDF. The closest book to this page's focus: every chapter ties back to data.
  • LAFF: Linear Algebra Foundations to Frontiers. Free, with exercises. Recommended by the CSE 6040 syllabus.
  • Khan Academy: Linear Algebra. Slower-paced videos with practice if a lesson here feels too fast.
  • Immersive Linear Algebra. A free online book with interactive figures.
  • NumPy linear algebra reference. solve, lstsq, eig, eigh, svd, qr.
